The World in One Page - Wednesday, 18 November 2026: 38 stories, date-checked. A front-page style poster that sums up the day's world news in one 1024x1024 image: the APEC summit in Shenzhen as the lead story, plus Asia-Pacific, Middle East, Europe, Americas, business and tech, and science headlines, a UTC sequence strip and the day in numbers. The poster is drawn by build_poster.py (Python, PIL); main.ts copies entry.png to the output, exactly as the judge will see it.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
